# Kookaburra Coffee Co. — Frequently Asked Questions

**When is my coffee roasted?**
We roast Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Order by 6am on a roast day and
your beans ship the same afternoon, roasted that morning.

**Do you grind coffee?**
Yes, free — choose espresso, stovetop, filter, or plunger at checkout.
Whole beans stay fresher, so we default to whole unless you ask.

**Is your decaf actually good?**
Featherweight is Swiss Water processed Colombian and it wins our staff
blind cuppings more often than anyone admits. $22.50 for 250g.

**Do you deliver to Perth?**
Yes — WA orders are $14.95 shipping and take 5–8 business days. We roast
WA orders to order so the coffee lands fresh despite the distance.

**Can I visit the roastery?**
Public cuppings run every second Saturday at 9:30am at our Marrickville
roastery. Free, but book ahead — the bench seats twelve.

**How do wholesale accounts work?**
Minimum 10kg per order with free Sydney metro delivery. Email
wholesale@kookaburracoffee.example for the current price list and samples.

**What's your sustainability story?**
Direct relationships with producers in PNG, Ethiopia, Colombia, and
Brazil; ACO-certified organic line; carbon-neutral AusPost shipping; and
roastery chaff goes to three Marrickville community gardens as compost.
