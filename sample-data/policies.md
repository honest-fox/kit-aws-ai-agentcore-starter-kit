# Kookaburra Coffee Co. — Shipping, Returns & Warranty

## Shipping

- **Sydney metro:** $7.95 flat, or free over $50. Typically next business day.
- **NSW regional / ACT:** $9.95, 2–3 business days.
- **VIC / QLD / SA / TAS:** $11.95, 3–5 business days.
- **WA / NT (including Perth and Darwin):** $14.95, 5–8 business days.
  Coffee is roasted to order for WA/NT shipments so it arrives fresh.
- We ship with AusPost carbon-neutral parcels. No international shipping yet.

## Returns

- **Coffee:** if you don't love a bag, tell us within 30 days and we'll
  replace it with a different roast or refund it — opened bags included.
  We'd rather you find a coffee you love than keep one you don't.
- **Equipment:** unused equipment in original packaging can be returned
  within **45 days** for a full refund. Used equipment with a fault is
  handled under warranty instead.
- Return postage for change-of-mind is at the customer's cost; faulty
  items ship back on us.

## Warranty

- Co-branded Comandante grinders carry a **5-year** manufacturer warranty
  serviced through us — email warranty@kookaburracoffee.example with your
  order number and a photo or video of the fault.
- AeroPress bundles carry the standard 1-year AeroPress warranty.
- Warranty claims are assessed within 3 business days of receiving the item.
