# Kookaburra Coffee Co. — Company Profile

Kookaburra Coffee Co. is a specialty coffee roaster founded in 2019 in
Marrickville, Sydney. We roast in small batches on a 15kg Probat and supply
both retail customers and around 40 wholesale cafe partners across NSW.

## Key facts

- **Founded:** 2019, Marrickville NSW
- **Founders:** Priya Raman and Tom Hollis
- **Team:** 14 staff (roastery, QC lab, and dispatch)
- **Roast days:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday — orders placed by 6am on a
  roast day ship the same afternoon
- **Certifications:** Australian Certified Organic (ACO) for the Green Ridge
  single origin line; Rainforest Alliance for all Papua New Guinea lots
- **Wholesale minimum:** 10kg per order, free metro delivery in Sydney

## Our sourcing

We buy directly from producer partners in Papua New Guinea (Eastern
Highlands), Ethiopia (Yirgacheffe), Colombia (Huila), and Brazil (Cerrado).
Green coffee is landed through the Port of Botany and stored in
climate-controlled GrainPro at our Marrickville warehouse.
